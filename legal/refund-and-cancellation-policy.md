This Policy explains when and how refunds, reversals, and cancellations
are handled for wallet top-ups, currency exchange orders, and withdrawal
requests made through the Global Exchanger app.

## 1. Wallet Top-Up (Add Money)

- Wallet top-ups are processed via Razorpay. Once a top-up payment is
  successfully verified and your wallet balance is credited, the top-up
  is considered complete and non-cancellable, as the funds have moved into
  your own wallet under your control.
- **If a payment is deducted from your bank/card/UPI but NOT reflected in
  your wallet balance** within 30 minutes:
  1. Do not attempt the payment again immediately — check your
     Transaction History first.
  2. Contact support at support@globalexchanger.com or raise a Complaint
     in-app with the payment reference/UTR number.
  3. We will investigate against Razorpay's transaction logs. If the
     payment was deducted but genuinely not credited to your wallet due to
     a technical failure on our end or the payment gateway's end, we will
     either credit your wallet or process a refund to your original
     payment method within **[INSERT SLA — e.g. 5-7 business days]** of
     confirming the failure, consistent with RBI's guidelines on failed
     transaction reversal timelines (TAT).
- If a payment fails or is declined by the bank/card network before
  reaching us, no funds are deducted and no refund action is needed —
  contact your bank if your statement shows otherwise.

## 2. Currency Exchange / Convert Orders

- Once a currency exchange order has been matched and completed
  (i.e., status shows "completed" and both currency legs have been
  updated in your wallet), the transaction is final and cannot be
  reversed, consistent with standard practice for currency conversion
  transactions and applicable exchange-rate risk.
- If an exchange order is still in "pending" or "escrow_locked" status
  and has **not yet been completed**, you may request cancellation via
  the in-app Support/Complaint feature. If approved, any escrowed/locked
  funds will be released back to your wallet balance in the original
  currency.
- If an exchange order fails due to a technical error on our end (e.g., a
  system fault preventing completion despite funds being locked), the
  locked funds will be released back to your wallet within **[INSERT SLA]**
  of the issue being identified.

## 3. Send Money (Peer-to-Peer Transfers)

- Send Money transactions between users are processed immediately and are
  **not reversible** by us once completed, as funds move directly into the
  recipient's wallet under their control.
- If you sent money to the wrong recipient or by mistake, see Section 8
  ("Wrong Payment") of our Terms and Conditions — we will make
  commercially reasonable efforts to assist (e.g., contacting the
  recipient) but recovery is not guaranteed and depends on the recipient's
  cooperation.

## 4. Crypto Conversion (BTC/ETH/XRP)

- Crypto conversion transactions, once completed, are **final and
  non-reversible**, consistent with the inherent nature of blockchain-based
  and crypto-linked transactions.
- Given the high volatility of crypto assets, we do not offer refunds or
  compensation for losses arising from price movement between the time you
  initiate and the time an order is executed/completed.

## 5. Withdrawals to Bank Account

- Once a withdrawal request is submitted, funds are locked in your wallet
  (shown as "locked" balance) and are unavailable for other transactions
  while the request is pending, approved, or processing.
- You may cancel a withdrawal request **only while it is still in
  "pending" status** (i.e., before our team has approved it) via the
  in-app Withdrawals screen. Once approved/processing, cancellation is no
  longer possible as funds transfer may already be underway.
- If a withdrawal request is rejected by our team (e.g., due to a bank
  detail mismatch or verification issue), the locked amount is
  automatically released back to your available wallet balance
  immediately.
- Processing time for approved withdrawals: **[INSERT YOUR ACTUAL SLA —
  e.g. "1-3 business days"]**, subject to bank processing times outside
  our control (NEFT/IMPS/UPI settlement windows, bank holidays).
- If a withdrawal is marked "completed" but you have not received funds in
  your bank account within **[INSERT SLA — e.g. 3 business days]**,
  contact support immediately with your withdrawal reference ID.

## 6. Commission and Fees

- Commission/fees charged on currency exchange transactions (currently up
  to 1%, as disclosed at the time of the transaction) are non-refundable
  once a transaction is completed, as this represents payment for services
  already rendered.
- If a transaction is cancelled before completion (per Sections 2-5 above),
  any commission that had not yet been deducted will not be charged; if a
  system error results in commission being deducted for a transaction that
  was subsequently reversed due to our fault, the commission will also be
  refunded.

## 7. Disputes and Chargebacks

- If you believe a charge was unauthorized or fraudulent, contact us
  immediately via the in-app Dispute feature or support@globalexchanger.com
  with full details, before initiating a chargeback with your bank/card
  issuer, so we can investigate promptly.
- We reserve the right to contest chargebacks that we can demonstrate were
  for legitimate, authorized, and completed transactions (e.g., supported
  by your Security PIN confirmation, device logs, and transaction records).
- Fraudulent or bad-faith chargeback attempts may result in account
  suspension pending investigation.

## 8. How to Raise a Refund Request

1. Open the app → Support → Complaints (or Disputes, if linked to a
   specific transaction).
2. Provide the transaction ID, date, amount, and a description of the
   issue.
3. You will receive an acknowledgement and status updates through the
   in-app Notifications feature.
4. If unresolved to your satisfaction, escalate via our Grievance
   Redressal Policy.
