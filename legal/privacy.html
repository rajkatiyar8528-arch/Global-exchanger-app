3. How We Use Your Data (Purpose Limitation)

We use personal data strictly for the following purposes, consistent with the DPDP Act's purpose-limitation principle:

  1. Creating and managing your account;
  2. Verifying your identity (KYC) before enabling payment, wallet, currency-exchange, crypto, or withdrawal features;
  3. Verifying bank account ownership for withdrawals;
  4. Processing payments, wallet top-ups, sends/receives, and currency/ crypto exchange orders;
  5. Detecting and preventing fraud, money laundering, and abuse (including of the referral program);
  6. Responding to complaints and disputes you raise;
  7. Complying with applicable law, regulatory requests, and law-enforcement requests (including reporting to FIU-IND under PMLA where required);
  8. Sending you transactional notifications (payment confirmations, KYC status, security alerts) via in-app notification and/or email;
  9. Improving the reliability and security of the Services (e.g., crash reporting, performance monitoring).

We do NOT use your personal data for unrelated purposes (e.g., we do not sell it to advertisers, and we do not use your KYC documents for any purpose beyond verification and legally required record-keeping).

4. Legal Basis / Consent

  • By registering and proceeding through KYC, you provide explicit, informed consent for us to collect and process the categories of data listed above for the stated purposes, as required under the DPDP Act.
  • Where we rely on "legitimate use" grounds recognized under the DPDP Act (e.g., compliance with a legal obligation such as KYC/AML law), we will clearly identify this at the relevant point of collection.
  • You may withdraw consent for optional/non-essential processing at any time (see Section 9), but withdrawing consent for data that is legally mandatory for a feature (e.g., KYC data for payment services) will mean that feature becomes unavailable to you.

5. Aadhaar-Specific Handling

  • We collect Aadhaar Card document images/scans (not live biometric authentication against the UIDAI database) as one accepted form of identity/address proof for KYC, consistent with the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 framework for voluntary use of Aadhaar as ID proof.
  • We do not perform live Aadhaar e-KYC/OTP-based UIDAI authentication unless and until we are separately authorised to do so as an AUA/KUA/ Sub-AUA entity; if this changes, this Policy will be updated to disclose the specific authentication flow and additional UIDAI-mandated disclosures.
  • Aadhaar numbers, where captured, are stored encrypted and access is restricted to authorized compliance/admin personnel only, and are never displayed in full within the admin panel except to personnel with specific KYC-review permissions.
  • We do not publish, share, or display your Aadhaar number to any unauthorized third party.

6. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Passwords and Security PINs are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) — we cannot see or recover your actual password/PIN.
  • KYC documents are stored in our database and access is restricted to administrators holding a specific "KYC management" permission role; all such access is logged in our audit log.
  • Communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • We apply rate-limiting, security headers, and monitoring to reduce the risk of automated attacks and unauthorized access.
  • No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required under the DPDP Act's breach notification requirements, without undue delay.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share necessary data, limited to what is required for the specific purpose, with:

  • Payment partners (e.g., Razorpay) — to process payments you initiate;
  • Banking partners — to process withdrawals to your verified bank account;
  • KYC/identity verification service providers, if/when we engage one, to assist verification;
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., our hosting provider) — solely to operate the Services, under contractual confidentiality obligations;
  • Email/SMS/notification service providers — to deliver OTPs, transactional notifications, and support communication;
  • Regulators and law enforcement — including RBI, FIU-IND, tax authorities, or courts, where legally required (e.g., PMLA reporting, Suspicious Transaction Reports, or a valid legal order);
  • Professional advisors (auditors, lawyers) — under confidentiality obligations, where necessary for compliance or dispute resolution.

We require any third party we share data with to protect it consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

8. International Data Transfers

If any of our infrastructure or service providers operate outside India, your data may be processed outside India. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure appropriate contractual and technical safeguards are in place, consistent with the DPDP Act's cross-border transfer provisions (which, as of the Act's current framework, permit transfers except to countries specifically restricted by the Central Government).

9. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities;
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • Erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to our legal obligation to retain certain financial/KYC/audit records for the periods described in Section 11;
  • Withdraw consent for processing that is based on consent (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal, and may make certain features unavailable);
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act;
  • Grievance redressal — file a complaint with our Grievance/Data Protection Officer, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@globalexchanger.com. We will respond within the timelines specified in our Grievance Redressal Policy (acknowledgement within 48 hours, resolution target within 30 days, or as required by applicable law).

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to meet legal, audit, tax, AML, fraud prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements. In particular:
