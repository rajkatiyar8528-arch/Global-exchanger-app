## 3. What Counts as a Grievance

You may raise a grievance for matters including, but not limited to:
- A transaction (send money, wallet top-up, currency exchange, crypto
  conversion, withdrawal) that did not complete correctly or as expected;
- Suspected unauthorized access to your account or a security concern;
- Disagreement with a decision made on your KYC, bank account
  verification, or withdrawal request;
- A complaint about customer support response time or quality;
- A request to access, correct, or erase your personal data, or any other
  right described in our Privacy Policy;
- Any other dissatisfaction with the Services not resolved through normal
  in-app Support/Complaint/Dispute features.

## 4. How to Raise a Grievance

You can raise a grievance through any of the following channels, in order
of preference (in-app is fastest for transaction-specific issues):

1. **In-app**: Support → Complaints or Disputes tab — provide transaction
   ID (if applicable) and a clear description.
2. **Email**: grievance@globalexchanger.com — include your registered
   email/phone, a description of the issue, and any relevant transaction
   IDs or screenshots.
3. **Post**: [INSERT REGISTERED ADDRESS], if email/app access is not
   possible.

## 5. Our Response Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |

If still unresolved, depending on the nature of your complaint, you may
also approach:

- **Banking Ombudsman / RBI Complaint Management System (CMS)** — for
  complaints related to payment processing, if the entity you're dealing
  with falls under RBI's regulatory ambit: https://cms.rbi.org.in
- **National Consumer Helpline / Consumer Forum** — for general consumer
  protection complaints, under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019:
  https://consumerhelpline.gov.in or 1915 (toll-free)
- **Data Protection Board of India** — for unresolved personal data
  grievances under the DPDP Act, 2023, once the Board's complaint
  mechanism is operational, per the Act's rollout schedule.
- **Cyber Crime Portal** — for suspected fraud or cybercrime:
  https://cybercrime.gov.in or dial 1930 (national cyber-crime helpline)

Nothing in this Policy prevents you from directly approaching any
statutory authority, consumer forum, or court at any time — the escalation
steps above are offered as faster, informal alternatives.

## 7. Special Note on Financial Transaction Grievances

For grievances specifically about a failed/delayed payment via our payment
gateway partner (Razorpay), our first step is to verify the transaction
status with the gateway before making a determination — this typically
takes 2-5 business days, consistent with standard payment gateway
reconciliation timelines. We will keep you updated in-app via
Notifications throughout this process.

## 8. Record-Keeping

All grievances raised, our responses, and resolution outcomes are logged
in our internal audit system for accountability, regulatory reporting, and
continuous improvement, and retained per our Privacy Policy's data
retention terms.
