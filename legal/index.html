Global Exchanger — Legal & Compliance Documents

These documents govern your use of the Global Exchanger app and services.

⚠️ These documents were drafted with AI assistance and should be reviewed by a qualified Indian lawyer before being relied upon as final, binding legal agreements. Bracketed placeholders inside each document must be completed by the business owner.

  • Terms and Conditions — the rules for using the app, KYC, payments, currency/crypto exchange, referrals, and dispute resolution.
  • Privacy Policy — what personal data we collect (including Aadhaar/PAN for KYC), why, how it's protected, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Refund & Cancellation Policy — when refunds/cancellations are possible for wallet top-ups, exchanges, and withdrawals.
  • Grievance Redressal Policy — how to raise a complaint, our response timelines, and escalation paths including RBI, Consumer Helpline, and the Data Protection Board.
