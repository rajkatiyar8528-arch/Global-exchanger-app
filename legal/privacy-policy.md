## 2. Personal Data We Collect

provide the Services:

| Category | Examples | Why we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| Identity data | Name, email, phone number, date of birth | Account creation, communication |

## 3. How We Use Your Data (Purpose Limitation)

We use personal data strictly for the following purposes, consistent with
the DPDP Act's purpose-limitation principle:

1. Creating and managing your account;
2. Verifying your identity (KYC) before enabling payment, wallet,
   currency-exchange, crypto, or withdrawal features;
3. Verifying bank account ownership for withdrawals;
4. Processing payments, wallet top-ups, sends/receives, and currency/
   crypto exchange orders;
5. Detecting and preventing fraud, money laundering, and abuse (including
   of the referral program);
6. Responding to complaints and disputes you raise;
7. Complying with applicable law, regulatory requests, and law-enforcement
   requests (including reporting to FIU-IND under PMLA where required);
8. Sending you transactional notifications (payment confirmations, KYC
   status, security alerts) via in-app notification and/or email;
9. Improving the reliability and security of the Services (e.g., crash
   reporting, performance monitoring).

**We do NOT use your personal data for unrelated purposes** (e.g., we do
not sell it to advertisers, and we do not use your KYC documents for any
purpose beyond verification and legally required record-keeping).

## 5. Aadhaar-Specific Handling

- We collect Aadhaar Card **document images/scans** (not live biometric
  authentication against the UIDAI database) as one accepted form of
  identity/address proof for KYC, consistent with the Aadhaar (Targeted
  Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act,
  2016 framework for voluntary use of Aadhaar as ID proof.
- We do not perform live Aadhaar e-KYC/OTP-based UIDAI authentication
  unless and until we are separately authorised to do so as an AUA/KUA/
  Sub-AUA entity; if this changes, this Policy will be updated to disclose
  the specific authentication flow and additional UIDAI-mandated
  disclosures.
- Aadhaar numbers, where captured, are stored encrypted and access is
  restricted to authorized compliance/admin personnel only, and are never
  displayed in full within the admin panel except to personnel with
  specific KYC-review permissions.
- We do not publish, share, or display your Aadhaar number to any
  unauthorized third party.

## 6. How We Store and Protect Your Data

- Passwords and Security PINs are stored using one-way cryptographic
  hashing (bcrypt) — we cannot see or recover your actual password/PIN.
- KYC documents are stored in our database and access is restricted to
  administrators holding a specific "KYC management" permission role; all
  such access is logged in our audit log.
- Communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  connections.
- We apply rate-limiting, security headers, and monitoring to reduce the
  risk of automated attacks and unauthorized access.
- **No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.** In the unlikely event of a
  data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify the Data
  Protection Board of India and affected users as required under the DPDP
  Act's breach notification requirements, without undue delay.
